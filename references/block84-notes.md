# Pass 84: Skeppsbron 30

Pass 84 replaces the generic two-storey district volume 91915619 with the low rendered building at Skeppsbron 30, west of the warehouse row. It has:
- a main body and a south-west wing, with pale ochre render, white corner pilasters, a plinth band and an eaves board;
- a dark hipped tile roof with three chimneys;
- on the north-east side towards Skeppsbron, a gabled porch with a double door, two windows and fretwork in its gable.

The panorama is a working reference only. No pixel is used as a texture.

## Measurement

The building was seen from one panorama on Skeppsbron, MHkw_ZTtIlSelRYJDaVShA, about 35 m away across a parking area. Heights and openings are estimates: eaves 3.6 m, ridge 7.0 m (wing 6.4 m), porch eaves 3.1 m and apex 5.2 m. The zones follow the OSM outline: the main body is 11.2 m deep from the north-east front, with the wing and the porch outside it.

## Verification

- The zones cover the outline: 372.8 m², with no gap and no overlap.
- The Blender geometry checks passed.
- Two rebuilds gave identical meshes.
- Passes 28–83 are unchanged.
- Model renders from the panorama position were compared with two views: the porch, the hipped roof and the chimneys agree.
- The Unreal import and its checks are deferred.

## Limitations

- **Estimates:** all heights and openings; the walls away from Skeppsbron carry generic windows.
- **Roofs:** they are boxed in the frame of the north-east front, so at the jagged south-east end they overhang the outline by up to 1.5 m.
