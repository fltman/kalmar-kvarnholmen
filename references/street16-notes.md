# Storgatan, pass 16

Nine west Storgatan footprint objects are revised, from Kaggensgatan to Larmtorget. Existing OSM footprints and inner courtyard cut-outs are retained. The eastern street and five previously authored square landmarks are outside this selective geometry pass.

Window openings are now cut into the outer masonry layer, with slim joinery and recessed glazing. These remain closed building exteriors, not enterable retail interiors. Three dedicated plaster materials reuse the project's original continuous fine-grain normal/roughness maps, replacing the unsuitable regular vertical seams in the pass-15 scan on these nine houses. Individually adjusted ochre, salmon and cream colors are visual estimates. Shared district materials and other houses remain unchanged. No new claim of current house-by-house survey accuracy or finished AAA quality is made.

## Performance method

Before/after captures use the same `36_Storgatan_Kaggensgatan` camera, unchanged lighting/scalability and editor viewport. Screenshots are disabled during the separate 30-second measurement; the parser discards the first eight seconds and requires actual rendered frames. This is an editor comparison, not packaged gameplay performance. Other Mac activity and shader/cache state can affect it.
