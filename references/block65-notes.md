# Pass 65: three 1940s houses on the north side of Fiskaregatan

Pass 65 details three district volumes on the north side of Fiskaregatan, opposite passes 58–60: 91846981, 91846966 and 91846970. They are matching two-storey houses of the 1940s:
- beige roughcast with salmon corner strips and a salmon band over the plinth;
- two axes of white-framed casements in white surrounds on each storey;
- a grey-green plinth with a barred cellar window under each ground window;
