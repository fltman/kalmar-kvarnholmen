# Pass 36: the cream house and the corner house on Södra Långgatan and Kaggensgatan

Pass 36 details OSM way 92379286, the last generic volume on the south side of this block of Södra Långgatan. The way holds two houses:

- **The cream house**, two storeys, next to the palace of pass 35:
  - on the ground floor, a modern shop front, an arched gate with boarded leaves, a tall window, a shop front of two windows and a door, and three tall windows;
  - eleven upper windows in light surrounds, two of them blind;
  - a band under the eaves and a red tile roof with three chimneys and small vents;
  - a curved gable with a round-headed window over the east end, with a copper roof behind it.
- **The corner house** on Kaggensgatan, in render with quoins, with two storeys and a portal dated 1659:
  - on both streets, tall shop windows under transom windows;
  - a grey band with iron wall anchors;
  - tall upper windows with brown casements in stone surrounds, a frieze and a cornice;
  - on Södra Långgatan, the sandstone portal:
    - steps up to an arched door;
    - rusticated piers and an entablature;
    - a broken pediment with the date cartouche.

The two houses are split at their measured joint. Each becomes a front range with lower ranges behind it.

Panoramas are working references only. No pixel is used as a texture, and no panorama, depth or tile data was extracted. A contributed photosphere on Kaggensgatan was not used.

## Geometry

- **The cream house.**
  - Walls in cream render over a grey plinth.
  - The barber's aluminium shop front.
  - The gate: a rectangular hole up to the crown with closed spandrel prisms, boarded leaves with a diamond pattern, a wicket, and a plain arch band and jambs.
  - Tall windows and the shop front in flat surrounds.
  - Upper casements in surrounds; blind panels on two axes.
  - The band, the eaves board and the gutter.
  - A saddle roof with fire walls to the palace and the corner house, three chimneys and three vents.
  - The curved gable:
    - built in vertical strips so that its outline stays single-valued;
    - scrolls at the shoulders, a cap, the round-headed window flush in its face;
    - a copper roof behind it, with cheeks down to the main roof.
- **The corner house.**
  - Render walls with quoins, the plinth and the band with six iron anchors on the street front (fleur-de-lis bars).
  - Shop windows and transom windows in stone surrounds, the upper casements, a frieze and a stepped cornice with the gutter.
  - The portal: rusticated sandstone piers, the arched door in a rectangular hole with closed spandrel prisms, the arch band, the entablature, the broken pediment with scrolls and the cartouche with a finial, and seven stone steps.
  - A hipped roof with a fire wall to the cream house, and a chimney.
- **Rear ranges.** The cream house's west end and the wing behind its east part: plain two-storey ranges under hipped roofs.
- **The neighbours' party walls.** The palace (pass 35) and the generic volume 92379292 behind were built against this way's old district height of 9.75 m. Two consequences:
  - the palace's west wall starts at 9.75 m;
  - 92379292 has no wall on the edges it shares with this way.

  The strips between the new, lower roofs and those walls are filled in this pass's mesh, on the neighbours' side of each party line. The palace's mesh is unchanged.

The 16 new `M_Block36_*` materials tint existing texture sets. There are no new texture sets.

## Verification

- **Zones:** `previews/block36-zones.json`. The three zones cover the outline exactly (586.1 m²), with no overlap and nothing outside OSM.
- **Build:** `previews/block36-build.json`. One mesh of 125,763 triangles, no zero-area UV triangles and no degenerate faces.
- **Repeatability:** `previews/block36-repeatability.json`. Two scoped rebuilds gave identical geometry, UVs and material slots.
- **Passes 26 and 28-35 unchanged:**
  - Their meshes in the saved blend match the repeatability reports of passes 28-35. All eight reports are byte-identical to the copies taken before the pass.
  - Their FBX files were not rewritten.
- **FBX audit:** `previews/block36-fbx-audit.json`. No zero-length or non-finite normals, tangents or binormals.
- **Unreal:**
  - 16 materials with normal and roughness maps;
  - the mesh imported with Nanite;
  - render buffers without zero or non-finite vectors.
- **Collision:** `previews/block36-collision.json`. 154 floor samples and 148 capsule sweeps along walking lines 1.2 and 2.0 m outside both houses' fronts on Södra Långgatan and Kaggensgatan and along the streets, with no floor failure. The portal's steps block both walking lines, as they should; each passes round them 0.8-1.25 m further out (a verified detour).
- **Calibration:** `previews/block36-calibration.json` compares 72 features in five views, read in side-by-side crops of the panorama and the Unreal review shot at the same angular scale.
  - Median deviation 3 px, largest 9 px; 71 of the 72 lie within 8 px.
  - The largest are:
    - the corner house's chimney top (9 px), estimated behind the cornice;
    - the cream house's west windows (5-7 px), which panoramas "18" and "17" place 0.1 m apart;
    - the portal's door crown and cornice (5-6 px).
  - Features outside the review shot's narrower 16:10 frame are dropped.
- **Delivery:** `previews/block36-delivery.json` collects all of the above; every report has passed.

**Corrected against the calibration views.**
- **The gate:**
  - 2.32 m wide, with its inner crown at 3.08 m (the first build had 2.23 m and a crown 0.07 m high);
  - its leaves stand nearly flush, since a deep reveal hid part of them in the oblique view.
- **The curved gable** moved 0.13 m east and 0.11 m forward, to the mean of its triangulated points. It had stood about 9 px off in the oblique view.
- **The portal's piers** went from 0.72 to 0.60 m wide. The door lost its light casing.
- **The corner house's gutter** is 0.45 m out, not 0.57 m.

## Reproduction

1. `KALMAR_GEO=… python3 scripts/prepare_block36.py`
2. In Blender, `scripts/rebuild_block36.py`. It re-runs the pass 26 and 28-35 builders for their helpers but exports only the pass 36 mesh.
3. `scripts/audit_block36_fbx.py` and `scripts/audit_block36_geometry.py` (run the latter after two rebuilds), then the pass 28-35 geometry audits.
4. Unreal, with `DEVELOPER_DIR=/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Developer`:
   - `resume_block36.py -HeroIdle`;
   - shots after the Nanite build;
   - then `{"execute":"validate_block36.py"}`.

Backup: `source/backups/block36/`.

## Limitations

- **The corner house's roof** is not seen from the street. Its hipped form and ridge (13.0 m) are estimates.
- **The corner house's Kaggensgatan front** is seen only at a grazing angle from the crossing.
  - Its axis positions are good to about ±0.3 m.
  - Its heights repeat those of the street front.
- **The cream house's roof** above the sky line, and both houses' rear ranges, are estimates.
- **Simplified:** the gable's volutes and cap, the portal's scrolls, cartouche and rustication, and the iron anchors.
- **Omitted:** tenant signs and window lettering, the barber's sign and pole, the parking and street signs, the date inscription on the portal, and the street furniture.
