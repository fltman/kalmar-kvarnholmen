# Northern street row: pass 21

Research and modelling date: 2026-09-20. These are exterior interpretations from historical street panoramas, not a measured architectural survey. No panorama pixels are used in the distributed geometry or materials. Footprints derive from the project's attributed OpenStreetMap data. New tile maps are original procedural work.

## Sources actually viewed

- [9 Norra Långgatan, Google, April 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@56.6637724,16.3619912,3a,90y,150h,100t/data=!3m4!1e1!3m2!1sZPJWrPQI4iykWC0dusWw3w!2e0): long two-storey cream house, green cross casements, red metal box dormers and panelled coach gate. The same panorama facing north shows the red/green infill shopping block.
- [12 Norra Långgatan, Google, April 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@56.6636395,16.3615327,3a,90y,332h,102t/data=!3m4!1e1!3m2!1stuH9AzqXSBpj7ovQdfTLKg!2e0): blue/green/red infill, grey connectors, tiled roofs and Köpmantorget passage. Facing south shows cream render with ceramic window aprons; facing southeast shows Gallerian's circular light, broad glazing, canopy and roof lanterns.
- [Kaggensgatan corner, Joakim Nilsson, June 2020](https://www.google.com/maps/@56.6638648,16.362379,3a,90y,30h,99t/data=!3m4!1e1!3m2!1sCIHM0ogKEICAgIDy3bfk3QE!2e10): rose plaster, white projecting bays and rounded shop awnings at the chamfered corner.
- [5 Norra Långgatan, Google, April 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@56.663455,16.3609409,3a,90y,28h,100t/data=!3m4!1e1!3m2!1smoIukgNuDhRLsn3U1E6G_w!2e0): modern white three-storey corner with brown frames and rooftop railing. Facing southeast shows the classical white three-storey corner with dark blue awnings. Temporary rooftop construction is omitted.

| 92204159 | North Gallerian entrance, circular light, glazed supported canopy, green roof lanterns | Existing Storgatan frontage preserved; roof join is an approximation |

Heights are estimated (roughly 7.3–11.2 m to eaves), not sourced cadastral measurements. Ground-floor glazing suggests depth but retail interiors are not reconstructed. Architectural signs are modelled lettering; tenant branding and advertising are omitted. Most rear elevations remain an interpretation. This pass does not mean all three streets are surveyed or that the world has reached AAA production quality.
