## Geometry and material decisions

Mapped building footprints and unaffected rear courtyard geometry are retained. The street faces replace old geometry inside explicit cut volumes, not overlay it. Timber battens stop at openings. Windows have recessed glazing, casing, hardware and metal sills. Roof planes, oriel side returns, awning supports, downpipe brackets and clipped plinths provide real depth; existing original normal/roughness textures add small surface relief.

Kullzénska and Areskogska are corner refinements on both street faces, not a claim that every house on Norra Långgatan, Kaggensgatan or Larmgatan has been individually reference-matched. Shop interiors and concealed rear elevations are approximate. No AAA completion or performance certification is claimed.
