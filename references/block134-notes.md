# Pass 134: the mainland around Ståthållaregatan, Gustaf Vasagatan, Vegagatan and Folkungagatan

Pass 98 built the mainland houses as plain volumes inside three chunk meshes. Pass 134 takes the 44 pass-98 outlines along Ståthållaregatan, Gustaf Vasagatan, Vegagatan and Folkungagatan out of those chunks. 43 of them become each its own mesh `SM_Slott134_<osm id>` (category `Slottsområdet/Mainland`, `detail_pass` 134), standing on pass 98's ground height (model z 0.30). The 44th, 93358501, is a cleared building site today and gets no mesh (see Selection).

The pass was started earlier as "pass 123", parked when that number went to the correction of pass 115, and is renumbered here. The parked files are still in `.pipeline/pending_streets123/` (see `MOVED_TO_134.txt` there). Every file name, function and global prefix, mesh name, material, `detail_pass`, JSON key and marker now carries 134; the parked zone data were regenerated by `scripts/prepare_block134.py`, not copied.

Each house gets:

- storeys (1, 1.5, 2, 3 or 4), the eaves and ridge heights, a stone plinth and, on houses over a basement, the basement windows;
- the roof: saddle, hip, Swedish mansard (gambrel, with gable ends), a hipped mansard (the yellow block's roof storey) or flat, in red or red-brown tile, dark tile or grey sheet metal;
- render in the house's colour, or vertical boarding with white corner boards; yellow brick on Vegagatan 4;
- eaves boards, white bargeboards on the gables, gable windows on the steeper gables;
- windows per storey (casement, surround, sill), white, red or dark frames; a door on the wall facing the street, steps where the door stands on the plinth;
- dormers, chimneys and the features in the table: the 1940s terrace rows on Ståthållaregatan with the white arched entrance bays, the arched door portal and the balconies over the doors; the villa pair's gabled dormers and red window frames; the yellow block's metal-clad roof storey with dormers on all four slopes; the yellow 1990s block's grey ground floor, white bay windows and red entrance canopy; the yellow block's balconies and the round-topped gable (its round window and the glass as triangle fans); the fire ladder; the half-timbered and boarded dormers on Folkungagatan; the grey garage row; the orange balconies and the glazed balcony tower on Vegagatan; the villas' central cross gables, porches with balconies, red awnings, the glazed balcony, the entrance canopies and the glazed top storey of the modern flat-roofed house.

## Selection

The selection is computed in `scripts/prepare_block134.py` from the OSM street lines in `references/osm-slott98.json` (ways with `highway` and `name`). It takes every outline in `source/block98.json['buildings']` that lies within 25 m of one of the four streets and whose nearest named street is one of them. It leaves out the Stagnell chapel 500979084 (pass 107) and the ids of passes 115–119 (read from their `source/block11x.json`). The later passes 120–133 touch none of these outlines (checked against every `source/block*.json`).

Twelve outlines within 25 m are nearer another street and are left to the passes of those streets; they are listed in `source/block134.json['left_to_other_streets']` (Frejagatan, Odengatan, Stensövägen, Stensviksvägen, Sankta Britas gata).

That gives 44 outlines. **93358501** (Gustaf Vasagatan 18; OSM: a white two-storey terrace, 14 zones) stands on a cleared, fenced building site in the April 2025 panorama (`gv06_h116`), and `gv03_h112` shows only the site fence at its place. It is kept in `source/block134.json['demolished']`: the build adds it to `SLOTT98_DETAILED`, so pass 98's chunk no longer draws its volume, and creates no mesh for it. This is a judgement on current imagery; to restore a volume, remove the id from `DEMOLISHED` in the prepare script.

The 43 built ids: 93238232, 93238235, 93238253, 93291958, 93291964, 93291971, 93291976, 93291992, 93291995, 93291998, 93292004, 93309094, 93309104, 93325620, 93326727, 93326736, 93326743, 93329939, 93329946, 93329966, 93361153, 93361164, 93361178, 93361208, 93361212, 93361215, 93361229, 93460154, 93460168, 93460186, 93460191, 93460202, 93460205, 93460212, 93460228, 93460239, 93477810, 93477827, 93477834, 94560085, 94560092, 94560096, 149134885.

**Chunks.** 38 of the 44 outlines lie in pass 98's chunk W (x < −1150) and six in M (−1150..−850); none in E. The build adds the 43 ids and 93358501 to `SLOTT98_DETAILED` (which keeps the ids of every earlier pass in the chain) and calls pass 115's `slott98_chunks115({'W','M'}, 134, block134_names)`. The two chunks are re-created by pass 98's code without these houses, keep `detail_pass` 98 and get `rebuilt_by_pass` 134. They are among this pass's meshes (45 names in all). E is not touched.

## Zones

Pass 116's slab method (as copied by pass 118):

- An outline that fills 85 % of its box in the frame of its longest edge is one zone; otherwise it is cut into slabs at its vertices and merged into a main body and wings, or flat-roofed annexes under 12 m² or narrower than 2.6 m (full height on blocks of three storeys or more).
- 93326736 has an explicit split read on `st03_h346` and `st02_h346`: the white villa pair covers the west 17.6 m of its street front (vertex 1 to 2), the yellow three-storey block with the hipped mansard roof storey the rest. Both ridges run along the street.
- The 1940s terrace row on Ståthållaregatan has its ridges along the street (`ridge: 'short'` on the row's zones).
- Each zone's walls follow the OSM outline exactly; walls against a neighbouring zone or house start at the neighbour's eaves.
- The door goes on the outer wall that faces one of the four streets; corner houses facing none use the nearest of Stensövägen, Frejagatan, Odengatan, Stensviksvägen, Sankta Britas gata, Johan III:s gata or Bremergatan.

## Limitations

- About fifteen houses have measured heights, most of them from the parked session with an assumed 2.3 m camera; only three cameras are resected. Ridge heights and roof pitches are estimated.
- 93358501 is left empty on the strength of one panorama (April 2025) that shows a cleared, fenced site. If new houses are built there, they are not in the model.
- The houses are boxes on the OSM outline with rectangular roofs; small notches are covered by the roof or by flat annexes at full height.
