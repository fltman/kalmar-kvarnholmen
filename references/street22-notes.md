# Kaggensgatan and Larmgatan northward — pass 22

Research date: 2026-09-20. Exterior modelling from the listed historical photographs, with estimated heights and obscured details. Reference pixels are not textures and are not redistributed.

## Sources actually viewed

- [34 Kaggensgatan, Google Street View, April 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@56.6645045,16.3617743,3a,90y,91h,103t/data=!3m4!1e1!3m2!1sZOtD0qfZ9ic42vX8HZeeQQ!2e0). The southeast corner is pale yellow/cream, three storeys, with a chamfered shop entrance, white cross casements on the middle floor, simpler paired upper casements and red metal dormers. North and west elevations are visible. Opposite, the shopping corner has exposed concrete framing, aggregate infill, paired top-floor windows, tall glazed projecting corner bays and blind middle panels. Its next north-facing section has rose aggregate infill.
- [Kaggensgatan 30, Corem property listing](https://www.corem.se/lediga-lokaler/kalmar/kalmar-centrum/kaggensgatan-30/), exterior photo `399692-305_001_red-1024x681.jpg`, accessed 2026-09-20. Eight modern upper window bays, beige render and narrow pale vertical strips, dark shop fascia, grey recessed doors, square dark-framed office windows and occasional blue awning cassettes. Publication/capture date is not confirmed by the filename.
- [32 Larmgatan, Google Street View, April 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@56.6637837,16.3605783,3a,90y,35h,113t/data=!3m4!1e1!3m2!1srca8D3E6xW5cscHihRzGDA!2e0). Three main storeys, red textured brick apron panels inside a white structural frame, groups of timber-framed square windows, narrow timber strips, projecting metal box windows on the upper floor, recessed shops and dark metal roof dormers. The north end has a taller attic window. The photographed fascia graphics and tenant names are omitted.

The basket-bond clinker PBR maps are original procedural work (`make_street22_textures.py`), with 250 mm groups, four-metre repeat, modelled mortar height and OpenGL normal maps. Their pattern interprets the photographed brickwork and is not a building scan. Heights are approximately 9.8–11.2 m to the main eaves. Interiors and unobserved elevations are not claimed to be reconstructed.
