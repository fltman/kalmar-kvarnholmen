# Pass 35: the wooden house and the palace on the south side of Södra Långgatan

Pass 35 details the two houses opposite the pass 34 houses, on the south side of Södra Långgatan. Each generic district volume becomes a front range on the street and lower ranges behind it.

- **The wooden house** (OSM 92379316; number 22 by its door), in ochre vertical boarding:
  - white corner boards and two white pilaster boards;
  - four shop windows, a glazed door, a red double door and a second glazed door in white casings, a narrow boarded panel with a vent, and a grey awning box over the first three openings;
  - a white band, then four upper windows with red casements in white surrounds under drip caps;
  - a white frieze and moulded cornice;
  - a broken red tile roof, steep over the eaves, with two wall dormers (round-headed windows in red fronts under segmental hoods) and a chimney on the ridge.
- **The palace** (92379296), three storeys in yellow render over a rusticated ground floor:
  - the stone portal with a pediment and a relief in its tympanum;
  - a narrow window, five shop windows, a glazed shop door and a wooden door, set in deep reveals under flat arches of radiating voussoirs;
  - a band, then two storeys of white casements between rusticated quoins;
  - a three-axis centre between two Ionic pilasters, under an entablature broken forward and a pediment with an oculus between garlands;
  - the cornice, three small roof dormers over each wing and a dark metal roof.

The long cream corner house to the west (92379286, on Södra Långgatan and Kaggensgatan) is left for a later pass.

Panoramas are working references only. No pixel is used as a texture, and no panorama, depth or tile data was extracted.

## Geometry

- **The wooden house.**
  - Boarded walls with battens; windows with red casements in flat white surrounds under thin drip caps.
  - The shop fronts in white casings, the doors and the boarded panel.
  - The band, the awning box and the upper windows in surrounds with drip caps.
  - The frieze, the stepped cornice and the gutter.
  - The broken tile roof in bands across the front range, with gables on both party walls, the ridge cap and the chimney.
  - The two wall dormers on the steep lower slope. Each front has a rectangular opening up to the springing and is built above it in strips that stop at the arch's trim; an arched hole's spandrels triangulate into slivers.
- **The palace.**
  - The ground floor: a 0.55 m thick wall with channelled rustication, the flat arches, the plinth, the band, the openings in their deep reveals and the portal (piers with consoles, the frieze, a cornice, and the pediment with its relief).
  - The upper storeys: casements set 0.13 m into the render without casings, the quoins, and the 0.70 m pilasters with their bases and Ionic capitals.
  - The entablature and cornice, broken forward over the centre, and the pediment with its oculus and garlands.
  - The roof: the cross gable behind the pediment, the six roof dormers and a dark metal roof.
- **Rear ranges.** Plain ranges with casements under inset roofs: two storeys behind the wooden house, three behind the palace.

## Reproduction

1. `KALMAR_GEO=… python3 scripts/prepare_block35.py`
2. In Blender, `scripts/rebuild_block35.py`. It re-runs the pass 26 and 28-34 builders for their helpers but exports only the pass 35 meshes.
3. `scripts/audit_block35_fbx.py` and `scripts/audit_block35_geometry.py` (run the latter after two rebuilds), then the pass 28-34 geometry audits.
4. Unreal, with `DEVELOPER_DIR=/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Developer`:
   - `resume_block35.py -HeroIdle`;
   - shots after the Nanite build;
   - then `{"execute":"validate_block35.py"}`.

Backup: `source/backups/block35/`.

## Limitations

- **Roofs and rear ranges.** The roofs above the eaves and the rear ranges are estimates. The palace's ridge comes from its east fire wall alone.
- **The palace's roof dormers.** Only their tops show above the cornice, so their height and depth are estimates.
- **The ground floor east of the palace's centre** is seen only obliquely, from both sides. Its openings' widths and their reveal depth are good to about ±0.2 m.
- **Registration.** The frame rests on the reported spacing of the panoramas and is checked by triangulation, not by each house's own joints. An error of 1 % in the spacing moves the cameras 0.2 m along the street.
- **Simplified:** the portal's relief, the garlands round the oculus and the capitals' volutes.
- **Omitted:** the portal's lettering, tenant signs and the lettering on the shop windows, the flag and the street furniture.
