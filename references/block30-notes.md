# Pass 30: the corner building Larmgatan 14 / Södra Långgatan

Pass 30 details the corner building at the north-east corner of the crossing of Larmgatan and Södra Långgatan (OSM 92204170, Larmgatan 14). It completes the south half of the block begun in pass 29.

The building has three storeys under a copper and tile mansard:
- a pink ground floor with stone-framed openings on a granite plinth, under a moulded band;
- ochre upper floors with red-brown casements;
- bracketed eaves with a red-brown soffit and a copper edge;
- a canted corner carrying the corner oriel;
- two further oriels, one on each front;
- two tall curved gable bays, one on Södra Långgatan and one at the north end of the Larmgatan front;
- fire walls above the lower neighbours to the east and north.

Larmgatan 16 (OSM 92204162) was not modelled. It is the pale yellow two-storey house north of the joint, and it can only be seen along Larmgatan at a grazing angle. There, the measurement put its base 0.76 m above the pavement, so it stays for a later pass.

Panoramas are working references only. No pixel is used as a texture, and no panorama, depth or tile data was extracted.

## Measurement

The method is pass 28's (see `references/block28-notes.md`).

**Södra Långgatan.** The camera stands 5.55 m from the facade, carried over from the pass 28 resection of the same camera track. It stands 2.32 m above the facade base. Its position along the street comes from the joint with Södra Långgatan 9, carried to it through a downpipe and two stone frames seen from both panoramas; those agree within 0.14 m.

**Larmgatan.** The panorama is registered on two points:
- the joint with Larmgatan 16, which falls on the left edge of the brown north gable bay;
- the canted corner's north end, seen as the silhouette from the north.

This puts the camera 5.24 m from the facade and 2.50 m above its base.

**The two bases differ.** The band runs round the corner at one height. It measures 5.36 m above the Södra Långgatan base and 5.53 m above the Larmgatan base, so the Larmgatan pavement lies 0.17 m lower. All heights below refer to the Södra Långgatan base.

**The canted corner has 1.7 m legs.** Four observations agree only with this:
1. Seen along Södra Långgatan from the east, the front ends 1.57 m short of the OSM corner.
2. In a magnified view from the south-west, the canted face measures 1.35 times its own shop window's frame. The building's other frames are 2.0 m wide, which makes the face 2.4-2.7 m.
3. From the north, the Larmgatan front's silhouette then falls 1.7 m short of the corner, which fixes the Larmgatan camera.
4. With that camera, the joint with Larmgatan 16 still falls on its OSM position.

## Geometry

- **Walls.** The pink ground floor, the ochre upper floors, the salmon Södra Långgatan bay and the brown north bay are split at the bay edges.
- **Openings.**
  - Stone-framed shop windows and doors with sill blocks.
  - Granite steps at the raised door on Södra Långgatan.
  - The carriage gate with an iron grille.
  - Upper casements with small panes.
- **Oriels.**
  - Oriels A and C are canted, 2.9 m at the wall and 1.6 m at the front, and project 0.75 m. Each stands on three stepped consoles with a carved apron: the arms of Kalmar on oriel A, a plain cartouche on oriel C.
  - Each has casements on both floors, a pediment hood between them and a copper bell cap with a finial.
  - The corner oriel's front is nearly as wide as the canted face. It carries the arms of Kalmar on its corbel and ends in a copper dome.
- **Gables.** Straight sides to the shoulders, an S-curve to a small segmental cap and a vase finial. The gable body runs back 4.6 m into the mansard, so its curved top reads as a roof from the side.
- **Eaves.** A red-brown soffit 0.8 m deep with rafter ends every 0.6 m, a fascia and a copper edge. They stop at the gable bays and at the corner oriel.
- **Mansard.**
  - A steep copper lower slope, a tile upper slope and a flat top.
  - The fire walls take no inset, so the roof rises straight from them.
  - At the upper slope's inset the canted face vanishes, and the two fronts' slopes meet in a hip over the corner.
- **Dormers and chimney.** Four copper dormers on the lower slope; the chimney at the east fire wall.

The 16 new `M_Block30_*` materials tint existing texture sets. There are no new texture sets.

## Verification

- **Zones:** `previews/block30-zones.json`.
  - The zone is the OSM outline less the 1.43 m² corner.
  - Both roof rings are valid polygons inside the outline.
- **Build:** `previews/block30-build.json`. One mesh of 109,929 triangles, no zero-area UV triangles and one degenerate face, which the export removes.
- **Repeatability:** `previews/block30-repeatability.json`. Two scoped rebuilds gave identical geometry, UVs and material slots.
- **Passes 26, 28 and 29 unchanged:** the pass 30 rebuild re-runs their builders for their helpers.
  - Their meshes in the saved blend still match `previews/block28-repeatability.json` and `previews/block29-repeatability.json`. Both reports are byte-identical to the copies taken before the pass.
  - Their FBX files were not rewritten.
- **FBX audit:** `previews/block30-fbx-audit.json`. No zero-length or non-finite normals, tangents or binormals.
- **Unreal:**
  - 16 materials with normal and roughness maps;
  - the mesh imported with Nanite;
  - render buffers without zero or non-finite vectors.
- **Collision:** `previews/block30-collision.json`.
  - 157 floor samples and 153 capsule sweeps along walking lines 1.2 and 2.0 m outside the Södra Långgatan front, the canted corner and the Larmgatan front, and along both streets.
  - No floor failure.
  - The one obstruction is the granite steps at the raised door on Södra Långgatan (0.53 m). A verified detour passes 0.8 m out.
- **Calibration:** `previews/block30-calibration.json` compares 58 features in five views. Each feature is read in the panorama, then marked on the Unreal review shot of the same camera.
  - Median deviation 5 px, largest 35 px; 49 of the 58 lie within 8 px.
  - The two largest are oriel C's bell cap and the corner dome, both in the steep view along Södra Långgatan. One window corner misread in a steep view is excluded; the level view confirms the modelled position.
  - The corner view (171) is not counted, since its camera was fitted to the model.
- **Delivery:** `previews/block30-delivery.json` collects all of the above; every report has passed.

## Reproduction

1. `KALMAR_GEO=… python3 scripts/prepare_block30.py`
2. In Blender, `scripts/rebuild_block30.py`. It re-runs the pass 26, 28 and 29 builders for their helpers but exports only the pass 30 mesh.
3. `scripts/audit_block30_fbx.py` and `scripts/audit_block30_geometry.py` (run the latter after two rebuilds), then `scripts/audit_block28_geometry.py` and `scripts/audit_block29_geometry.py`.
4. Unreal, with `DEVELOPER_DIR=/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Developer`:
   - `resume_block30.py -HeroIdle`;
   - shots after the Nanite build;
   - then `{"execute":"validate_block30.py"}`.

Backup: `source/backups/block30/`.

## Limitations

- **Roof.** The roof's flat top and the courtyard slopes are estimates. The street slopes follow the east fire wall's profile, and the dormer positions are approximate.
- **Canted corner.** It rests on four consistent but indirect observations. Its legs are good to about ±0.3 m.
- **Corner view (171).** Its camera was fitted to the model itself (2.3 m from the reported position), so it serves only as a review view.
- **Oriel caps.** The heights of oriel C's bell cap and of the corner dome are uncertain by about 0.5 m.
- **Courtyard fronts.** They are plain.
- **Omitted:** tenant signs, the ticket agency's valances and the hanging sign by the gate.
- **Larmgatan 16** is not modelled.
