# Castenska garden / Gerda portal refinement

Reference: https://www.kalmarkusten.se/platser/castenska-garden/ (exterior and portal photographs). Photos remain research-only, excluded from publication.

Continuous wave-cut pilasters, moulded capitals, supported finials, oval cartouche and inset arched timber joinery replace the earlier segmented approximation. Door panels and hardware are inferred; the face relief is stylised, not a scan. First-floor sill height is adjusted to clear the upper stone finial; dimensions remain estimates.

Original tileable stone base colour, roughness and OpenGL normal maps are generated by `scripts/make_portal19_texture.py`. A repeat spans 0.5 m; material UV scale 8 compensates for the mesh's 4 m UV projection. No pixels from the reference photographs are embedded.

Regenerate textures with system Python, then run Blender in background with `scripts/rebuild_portal19.py`. In Unreal run `refresh_portal19.py`; remove its completed `previews/portal19-import.json` checkpoint before intentionally reimporting a later revision.

Varmbadhuset also receives continuous shaped gable copings, curved square copper caps, sealed arched timber reveals, a supported entrance statue and a stone frieze carrying BADHUS. Facade bands are stopped at the entrance and niche. Main reference: https://www.kalmarkusten.se/platser/gamla-varmbadhuset/ . The statue remains schematic; this pass does not claim a faithful sculptural reconstruction.
