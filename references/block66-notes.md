# Pass 66: Norra Långgatan 49

Pass 66 details the district volume 91885531 on the north side of Norra Långgatan, east of the 1964 block (91885540, not detailed). It is a salmon rendered three-storey house:
- a rusticated ground floor on a dark granite plinth, four tall brown-framed windows with dark sills, and the round-arched gateway (49) with panelled wooden doors at the west end;
- a cream cornice over the ground floor;
- on the first floor, a grouped triple window under one entablature and single windows over the gateway and at the east end, each over a carved apron between consoles;
