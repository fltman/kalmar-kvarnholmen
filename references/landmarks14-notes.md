# Landmark and fortification pass 14

This pass replaces eight pre-existing mapped building objects and adds surviving fortifications. Metre footprints come from OpenStreetMap; facade dimensions, roof pitches, some rear elevations and decorative carving are interpreted rather than measured.

## Visual references actually reviewed

- Tripp-trapp-trull: Wikimedia photo `Kalmar, Fiskaregatan, Tripp-trapp-trull-husen, 2015b.jpg`, viewed locally. Low green timber gable, taller cream plaster gable, tallest red timber gable; ochre sashes and white verge boards. OSM and published names disagree about individual house labels, so use the ensemble name.
- Varmbadhuset at Kattrumpan: Wikimedia `Kalmar, Gamla varmbadhuset, Kvarnholmen, 2015a.jpg`, and Google Street View September 2009 at 56.6651698,16.3717613, heading120, panorama `sG5fJXiSyJ9I_9cKxr1PUQ`. Viewed the portal, west oriel, gables, red roof and small copper towers. Sculpture is a simplified interpretation.
- Larger old fire station: Kalmar läns museum, *Brandstationen i Kalmar*, April2012, pp29–30, plus Google Street View June2025 at56.665642,16.3597736 heading140, panorama `rKBf2EXlgpPpGcwYy3buRA`. The current facade is cream/yellow; the older apricot colour was not carried over. The modern western wing has been altered since2012.
- Ångkvarnen: Google Street View April2025 at56.663853,16.3706393 heading330, panorama `s5EdUU8_As7hXsySND896A`; Wikimedia `Kalmar läns museum (f.d. Kalmar Ångkvarn), 2015a.jpg` and `Kalmar läns museum (f.d. ångkvarnen i Kalmar) i juli 2009.JPG`. Reviewed harbour elevations, brick piers and buff fields, glazing, tower crenellations and pale western entrance wing. Storey heights and the internal distribution of roof volumes are estimates.
- Fortifications: Kalmar läns museum, *Kvarnholmens befästningsverk – Bevarande- och utvecklingsplan*,2020. Reviewed plans pp23,75,107,139 and stonework photos pp59,63,65,117,126,127,130; text on dimensions, reconstructed low eastern curtains and surviving north-wall fragments. OSM city-wall ways91066703,91931331,91931333,91931338,698954011,698954012 supply alignments.

## Scope and limits

The scene represents the surviving walls, not an invented unbroken seventeenth-century ring. It includes Johannes Rex/south rampart, Regeringen, Carolus Philippus, eastern curtain/Holmporten gap, western walls, Muren and the low Lustgården trace. Västerport, Jordbroporten and Kavaljersporten have open vaulted passages. Ground outside these earthworks retains the previous simplified district elevation. Mapped roads are cleared through the new masonry; this is a gameplay accommodation where elevation data are missing. The separate Ravelinen Prins Carl is documented but requires further georeferencing before addition.

Materials are original procedural 2048px base-colour, normal and roughness maps, with a four-metre repeat: red and buff brick, rough stone with recessed joints, lime plaster, olive wood, ochre joinery, aged copper turf and dressed ashlar. Reference photographs are visual research only and are not facade texture assets. Existing church and previously reviewed street assets are preserved.
