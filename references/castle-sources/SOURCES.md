# Kalmar slott: sources for the approach and the interior

Research date: 2026-10-02. This file is a catalogue of sources only. No model code was written.

It builds on `references/castle27-notes.md` and `references/castle27/`. Pass 27 already holds:
- the OSM extract
- Zettervall's west elevation of 1883
- the -wuppertaler photographs, including `swe-kalmar-slott-006.jpg` of the well house
- the Street View calibration, which was viewed only.

Nothing here comes from Google imagery.

## Contents

- **Downloaded files.** The images in this folder are listed in `sources.json` with their page URL, author, date and licence. There are 34 of them, about 32 MB in total.
  - Files named `commons-*` are thumbnails 2000 px wide from Wikimedia Commons. Their licences are PD, CC BY 3.0/4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0 and CC0.
  - Files named `dimu-*` are 2000 px derivatives from DigitaltMuseum (Kalmar läns museum), marked by the museum with the Public Domain Mark ("Utgången skyddstid").
- **Listed but not downloaded.** Everything else is only listed, because its rights are unclear or not stated, or because it could not be reached.

## Important findings

### Best plan drawing

The best plan drawing with a scale is **Martin Olsson, Fig. 1 in "Tornen, de kungliga bostadsvåningarna och Gamla kyrkan i Kalmar slott I", *Fornvännen* 69 (1974), pp. 89–104, on p. 91.**
- **What it shows.** "Plan av Kalmar slott och huvudvåningen omkring år 1574". This is the whole island inside the bastioned rampart, plus the main floor of the castle.
- **Scale and orientation.** It has a graphic scale bar from 0 to 100 m and a north arrow.
- **Room numbers.** Rooms carry Olsson's numbering:
  - 55 Kungstrappan
  - 56 Kuretornet's upper room
  - 59 Gyllene salen
  - 61a and 61b, which became Grå salen (61)
  - 62 Gamla Kungsmaket
  - 63 Rutsalen
  - 65 Drottningsalen
  - 66 Drottningens övre förstuga and Drottningtrappan
  - 73 a room in Fångetornet
  - 74 and 76 Stora östra salen, later Förbrända salen
  - 87 Stora västra salen, later Gröna salen
  - also 68, 72, 75, 77, 82, 84, 86 and 88.
- **Tower numbers.** Towers are I Kuretornet, II Kungsmakstornet, III Vattentornet, IV Norra trapptornet (Svennetornet), V Fångetornet, VI Nordöstra, VII Sydöstra, VIII Södra, IX Rödkullatornet, X Västra porttornet, XI Norra yttre vattenporten and XII the small tower by Kungsmaket.
- **URL.** <https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1225150/FULLTEXT01.pdf>. It is a PDF of 17 pages with an OCR text layer.
- **Rights.** The PDF is open access on DiVA, but the record states no licence. Fornvännen's CC BY 4.0 applies to current articles, and the archive's terms are not stated. Martin Olsson died in 1981, so the drawing is in copyright until the end of 2051. **It was not copied into the project.** Fetch it from DiVA when it is needed.
- **Use.** It is the only plan found with a scale that also names the rooms. Register it to OSM on the four corner towers and Kuretornet. Its scale bar gives a check on the drawing's own scale.
- **Measurements in the text:**
  - Rutsalen (63) measures 11 × 10.5 m.
  - Stora östra salen (74 and 76) is about 440 m².
  - Room 73 is about 45 m².
  - A barrel-vaulted cellar under room 2 is 5.5 × 3.4 m.
  - The cellar vault under the north range is "13 m wide and fairly flat", quoting Åkerlund in Fv 73.
  - The prison floor in Vattentornet lay 1.7 m above mean water level in Slottsfjärden in 1930.

### Correction to pass 27: the well house is hexagonal

Martin Olsson describes the courtyard well in "Kalmar slotts portaler och brunnsbyggnaden på slottets borggård", *Fornvännen* 1957, pp. 137–183. The URL is <https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1224675/FULLTEXT01.pdf>, a 48-page PDF with an OCR text layer and no licence stated.

His description:
- **Well shaft.** It is cylindrical and domed, about 1.5 m in diameter, and built of dressed Öland limestone.
- **Parapet.** It has **six** engaged pedestals with lion and goat masks. Its panels carry cartouches and the Three Crowns arms.
- **Order and roof.** Smooth Doric columns on the pedestals carry an entablature that is **hexagonal in plan**, with a triglyph frieze and "diamond" metopes. Above it are **six** low pediments with busts. Six curved brackets with mascarons rise from its corners to a moulded slab. On the slab stands a lantern of **six** herm pillars with Ionic capitals, under a domed roof with six round-topped gables.
- **Finial.** A cast-iron dolphin standing on its nose was added in the mid-1800s. It replaced a stone lion holding a shield.

Wikimedia Commons also files the well under "Hexagonal fountains".

**Pass 27 modelled an octagonal base with eight columns and four pediments, which should be corrected.** Count the columns per corner from `castle27/swe-kalmar-slott-006.jpg` and `dimu-021017055878.jpg`. Olsson implies one column per pedestal, but the photographs appear to show more shafts.

### The gate building in the dry moat

Olsson's caption of 1934 (`dimu-021017066800.jpg`) reads, in translation: "the masonry of the hemliga värnet has been conserved and its drawbridge rebuilt". The low rubble-stone wall with small openings at water level is therefore the **hemliga värnet**, a covered fausse-braie or caponier on the west front. This is what pass 27 called "the north-west lower wall with brick-arched casemate openings".

The photograph shows:
- the timber trestle bridge
- the drawbridge frame with its lifting beams, in front of the round-arched gate (portal A) in the battered west rampart
- Kuretornet behind.

## A. The approach

### Route and names, after Olsson 1957 (fig. 1 on p. 139, a plan without a scale)

1. **Bridge across Vallgraven**, from the ravelin. The present timber bridge and drawbridge were rebuilt in the 1930s after the moat was dug out in 1932–34. Before that a road embankment ("Slottsbrobanken", "vägbank") led in.
2. **Portal A.** A round-arched entrance through the west rampart, "ingången genom vallen", leads to the outer bailey. A coat-of-arms tablet from Roland Mackle's workshop of 1568 sits over it, with the Three Crowns held by two crowned lions and a triglyph frieze. A reconstruction of the whole portal is in Olsson's *Ritningar*, pl. 50.
3. **A vaulted passage under the rampart**, "den välvda gången under vallen", into **Västra förborgen**, the outer bailey, also called "yttre borggården". Gravel and stone setts in the bailey belong to the old drive up the rampart.
4. **Portal B**, at the outer mouth of the gate passage (portgång) through the castle. It has two fluted Doric columns on tall plain pedestals and an entablature with triglyphs and metopes, alternately an ox skull and a round shield.
5. **The gate passage** through the west range at Kuretornet, into the inner courtyard.
6. **Portal C**, "Västra borggårdsportalen", at the courtyard end. It is the richest portal: four pairs of fluted columns, Ionic below and Corinthian above, and herms carrying the arms tablet with Johan III's crowned initials "I R".
7. **The courtyard** has further portals:
   - D, Drottningtrappan in the north range, rusticated with banded Doric columns
   - E, Kungstrappan in the west range, now fragmentary
   - F, Kyrkportalen in the south range, dated 1568, with herms
   - G, the south-west portal of the walled-up south gate passage
   - H, in the wall between Fångetornet and Östra postejen, outside the courtyard.

## B. The interior, room by room

These names are confirmed for Kalmar: Gyllene salen, Grå salen, Kungsmaket (Kungsgemaket), Rutsalen, Drottningsalen, Gröna salen, Förbrända salen, Slottskyrkan, Agdakammaren and Kungsköket.

The brief also listed two names that were not found:
- **Rikssalen.** No Kalmar source uses this name. DigitaltMuseum's "Rikssalen" hits are Borgholm and Örebro.
- **Gustav Vasas gemak.** No Kalmar source uses this name either.

"Drottningens gemak" corresponds to Drottningvåningen in the north range, with Drottningsalen (65) and Rutsalen (63).

### Kungsmaket or Kungsgemaket (room 62, in Kungsmakstornet)

**Well documented.**
- **Features.** Erik XIV's chamber of about 1560, with intarsia panelling and Corinthian columns. Its coffered ceiling has the king's initials and the date 1562, and Johan III added a hunting frieze in 1572–73.
- **Panel height.** The panelling is about 2.6 m high. This figure comes from secondary sources (Kalmar slott, Wikipedia) and should be verified.

### Gröna salen (room 87, Stora västra salen, in the south-west range, next to Rödkullatornet)

**Good for the shell.** The room is a large hall with:
- a wide-board floor
- an exposed beamed ceiling
- deep window niches with painted soffits
- a stone door surround
- funeral escutcheons on the walls.

### Förbrända salen (rooms 74 and 76, Stora östra salen, in the east range, about 440 m²)

**Moderate documentation.**

- Photos from 1922 show it as a museum hall (021016517088 and 517090).
- Photos from 1970 record the work on the floor joists (021017090070–090082).
- No modern photograph with a stated licence was identified. The Commons "Kalmar castle and exhibits" series by Netha Hussain (CC0, 2021) and Maria Eklind's set (CC BY-SA 2.0) are of unnamed rooms. Sort them by eye.

### Slottskyrkan (south range, 1589–92)

**Very well photographed.**
- **Commons.** The category "Interior of Kalmar slottskyrka" has 53 files: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Interior_of_Kalmar_slottskyrka>.
  - 17 photos by Jopparn (2015, CC BY-SA 4.0), including the panorama `Slottskyrkan i Kalmar slott 08.JPG`, which is saved here.
  - Baboş's `Kalmar slott.slottskyrka.jpg` (CC BY 3.0), saved here.
  - 25 RAÄ/KMB photos from 1919–1968 (public domain), interiors looking east and including the pulpit and chancel. One is by Martin Olsson (1919).
- **DigitaltMuseum.** Dagmar Selling's 1969 restoration series is PDM. It records each wall, the painted pews, the king's and queen's pews, the pulpit and the chancel wall (021017083403–090219).
- **Gap.** No plan or dimensions were found, and the chapel's length needs a plan.

### Rutsalen (63) and Drottningsalen (65), north range, Drottningvåningen

**Thin.**
- Rutsalen measures 11 × 10.5 m (Olsson 1974).
- Kulturvärden 1/2025 describes Rutsalen's painted and gilded coffered ceiling and its intarsia panelling: <https://www.sfv.se/kulturvarden/artiklar/kulturvarden-1-2025/kungligt-i-kalmar>. The photographs there are by Marcus Funke/Maddoc and are © SFV.
- No openly licensed interior photograph was identified. A DigitaltMuseum search for Rutsalen and Drottningsalen gave 131 hits, mostly without a licence.
- 021017034412 (1915, PDM) shows the wooden portal into Drottningsalen from Norra vindelstenen.

### Kungsköket (room 43, ground floor, east range)

**Well recorded in the 1969–74 restoration photos (PDM):**
- Selling's 1969 series shows each wall, the large fireplace, the flue and the drain (021017090083–090103).
- K. Pettersson's 1972–74 series shows the large and small fireplaces and the reopened doorways.
- The ground-floor sketch of 1969 is listed under plans above.

These are photographs of a work site, not of a finished interior.

### Agdakammaren, Kuretornet's rooms and the Frökenrummen

**Thin.**
- **Agdakammaren.** It adjoins Gyllene salen. Four photos from 1974 show the floor insulation; no licence is stated.
- **Kuretornet.** One PDM interior from 1944 is 021017090112. The 1975 detail photos of floors I–V (021017083316–090030) have no licence stated.
