# Pass 92: Landshövdingegatan, west side, north of Storgatan

Pass 92 replaces five generic district volumes from pass 17 with the houses that stand there. The volumes are split into nine zones.

From Storgatan northwards along the west side of Landshövdingegatan (street fronts facing east, except the first, which faces south onto Storgatan):

- **93192387 (the corner house):** an olive-green boarded two-storey house on Storgatan from x 282.74 to 294.91. It has four windows below and five above, all in white frames, and white corner boards. At the west end of the front is a white carved double door with grilles, flanked by pilasters, under a cornice, on one step. The roof is a red tiled saddle roof with a red brick chimney and a grey one. The east gable on Landshövdingegatan (y 0.07–7.5) has two windows a floor, a pair of small attic windows and white bargeboards.
  - Behind the main roof, from y 7.5 to 9.7, is a low boarded strip under a lean-to. On Landshövdingegatan it shows a dark side door on steps and a small window.
  - The west end of the outline (x 280.19–282.74) is the gate section that pass 91 left for this pass: pale green boarded gate leaves to 2.1 m, a grey beam, and a yellow boarded panel above to 3.6 m.
- **93192432:** an ochre rendered three-storey house (y 9.69–19.29). It has white corner pilasters on dark brown bases, a dark brown plinth, a dark red gateway door at the south end, three windows below and four above in dark brown frames. The street gable is a gambrel: the walls rise to 6.1 m, steep lower slopes reach 8.3 m 1.1 m in from each side, and the top is nearly flat at 8.45 m. Two attic windows sit in the gable, and the back gable repeats the profile.
- **93192395:** a three-and-a-half-storey stucco house (y 19.29–29.49).
  - The ground floor is brown-pink on a grey plinth. It has the arched gateway at the south end with a white archivolt, a white passage and the yard gate seen at the far end. There are also two windows with round-headed surrounds and two glass-block cellar lights.
  - A band runs at 4.4 m. Above it, the upper floors are beige-pink, with three windows a floor in white surrounds and two iron wall anchors.
  - The roof is a red tiled saddle with a chimney on the north gable.
- **93192444:** a red boarded house with its gable to the street (y 32.65–39.11). It has two windows a floor in ochre frames and red bargeboards, and a lower red back wing to the west.
  - The carved double gate stands in the 3.2 m gap between this outline and 93192395 (y 29.49–32.65). The gap is outside the OSM outlines, so the gate is built as part of this mesh.
  - The gate has orange-brown leaves with raised panels and lozenges under a red lintel panel. Its red boarded wall rises with the house's south roof slope from 3.9 m to the eaves, and a tiled slope covers the passage behind it.
- **93192382:** a pink boarded two-storey house (y 39.11–49.25). It has light pink pilaster boards at the corners and between the bays, three windows below and four above, and a dark plinth with a cellar hatch.
  - At the north end is the grey-green double door, with a transom and a panel above under a light surround.
  - The roof is a red tiled saddle with a chimney. The low back wing sits in the outline's south-west part.

Google Street View imagery is a view-only reference. No pixel is used as a texture.

## Measurement

Four panoramas from April 2025 were used. All are 696×375 crops with a vertical field of view of 90° and a pitch of 15°.

**Method.**
- Each camera was resected from two building corners on the facade plane, 0.355 m in front of the OSM line. The plane through the outline itself was not used.
- The camera height is the pavement line read on that plane.
- Heights are read on the same plane and are given in metres above the pavement.

**Which outline is which.**
- The heights of the four resected cameras all come out between 2.3 and 2.44 m. That agreement is the check on the identification.
- *65 Storgatan.* Resecting on the full OSM front (x 280.19) gave a camera 2.95 m high. Resecting on pass 91's joint at x 282.74 gives 2.44 m. The 2.1 m gate west of the house therefore sits inside the outline of 93192387, as pass 91 found.
- *16 Landshövdingegatan.* Two readings of the north end were possible:
  - with the grey double door inside 93192382 (north edge y 49.25), the camera comes out 2.43 m high and the door 2.3 m tall;
  - with the door in the gap up to 93192433 (y 52.43), the camera comes out 3.2 m high and the door 3.2 m tall, which is implausible.

  The door is therefore part of the pink house. In the photo the yellow house (93192433, pass 81) adjoins the door directly, but its OSM outline starts 3.2 m further north. That discrepancy is outside this pass.
- *12 Landshövdingegatan.* The carved gate reads y 29.47–32.36, which is the gap between the outlines. The red house north of it (y 32.65–39.11) is 93192444.

## Estimated

- Everything not seen from the street: depths, rear walls and their generic windows, the roofs behind the fronts, the back wings of 93192444 and 93192382, the flat roof of the gate section, and the passage and yard gate behind the stucco house's arch.
- The stucco house's eaves (11.4) and ridge (15.6). The 12 panorama shows the facade only to 10.7 m. The north gable's rake, read from the 16 panorama at a distance of about 17 m, gives the ridge.
- The gambrel's top (8.45) on the ochre house.
- The red house's eaves and apex. The near (12) and far (16) panoramas disagree by about 1 m on the rake heights; the near one was preferred.
- Chimneys, window widths (from frames, without the surrounds) and colours (sampled from the photos, then lightened for the shade).

## Verification

- `scripts/prepare_block92.py` prints `BLOCK92_ZONES_OK`. The zones cover the five outlines: 552.0 m² in all, 0.07 m² not zoned, 0.02 m² outside OSM and 0.015 m² overlap.
- **The sandbox build** (`SCR/sandbox.py` on a copy of `source/Stortorget.blend`, prelude pass 90) prints `BLOCK92_GEOMETRY 5` and `SANDBOX_DONE` without errors.
  - `drop_degenerate_faces92` (zero area, repeated corner, slivers under 0.1 mm) is called right after `s21_finish`. It removed 22, 16, 14, 44 and 18 faces from the five meshes.
  - Renders with and without the drop were not compared.
- **Renders from the resected cameras**, compared side by side with the photographs in `SCR/p92/sb2/cmp_c387.jpg`, `cmp_c432.jpg`, `cmp_c395.jpg` and `cmp_c382.jpg` (aerial: `SCR/p92/sb1/aer.png`):
  - *The corner house (65 Storgatan):* the front's ends, the eaves, both window rows, the door and the gate section line up within a few pixels.
  - *The ochre house (9 Landshövdingegatan):*
    - The pilasters, gate, windows, knees and gambrel break line up.
    - In the first iteration the top rose to a visible apex. It was lowered to 8.45 m, nearly level, as in the photo.
    - The green gable to the left (bargeboard foot, small window and side door) matches.
  - *The stucco house (12 Landshövdingegatan):*
    - The arch, the windows of all three floors, the band, the plinth with the cellar lights and the anchors match.
    - The ochre house's top and corner edge to the left match.
    - The red gate's position and height match. Its carving is simplified.
  - *The pink house (16 Landshövdingegatan):*
    - The front's ends, the windows, the pilaster boards and the door match.
    - The first iteration's eaves (6.6) sat about 10 px too high. They were lowered to 6.2, which matches the photo's eaves line.
    - The stucco house's north gable and chimney, seen behind the red house, fall within about 15 px.
- The official build and the Unreal checks are pending; the lead fills in the build results.

## Limitations

- The rear walls and roofs behind the fronts are estimated; see above.
- 93192433 (pass 81's yellow house) adjoins the pink house's door in the photograph. Its OSM outline, however, starts 3.2 m further north. This leaves a gap in the model between y 49.25 and 52.43 that is not there in reality.
- The OSM joint between the corner house and the ochre house (y 9.69) may be 0.2–0.3 m too far south: the ochre house's south pilaster reads at y 10.0–10.9. The joint was kept.
- The stucco house's ground windows are drawn with rectangular openings; only their surrounds are round-headed.
- **Omitted:** the carvings on the red gate (simplified to panels and lozenges), the lanterns at the arch, the street lamp, signs, house numbers, downpipes, the plants and the antennas.
- **A view that would help:** the stucco house's eaves and roof, from about (301, 12), heading 242, pitch 35.
