# Known limitations and useful next contributions

- Most background facades are inferred from footprints and broad height estimates. Having windows on every mesh is not the same as faithfully reconstructing every real building.
- Heights and small carvings are generally estimated. Sculpture details are stylised, not scans.
- Västerport's exterior has reference-based detailing, but its tunnel still simplifies the real bent passage.
- Parking polygons are mapped; painted bays are schematic 2.5 × 5 m cells with 6 m aisles, not an inventory of real markings or parking rules. Grass surfaces need more vegetation and edge treatment.
- The cathedral's public-edition altar canvas is neutral: the earlier photo is excluded for license compatibility. A compatible photograph or original historically supported reconstruction is welcome.
- Shop signs are approximate and may depict older photographed appearances. No current business inventory is implied.
- Lumen / Nanite are enabled, but the scene is not performance-optimised or packaged. A previous broad district editor capture was about 15 FPS on the development machine; this is not a target performance claim.
- Only the recorded macOS authoring environment has been tested. Windows/Linux testing, portable launchers and a reproducible end-to-end regeneration workflow are useful contributions.
- Large binary files live in versioned GitHub release bundles and are difficult to merge. Coordinate edits to the shared Blender file and Unreal map; share changed assets through a fork release linked from your PR.
- Passes 23–25 (Baronen, the Kalmar C station house, the station's tracks and platforms) are applied by their scoped rebuild scripts on top of the released scene; `scripts/build_kvarnholmen.py` alone does not reproduce them. Railway turnouts have no switch blades, frogs or point machines, and the overhead messenger wire has no sag.
- Passes 26–27 (the Larmgatan houses by the station, Kalmar slott on Slottsholmen) are likewise applied by `scripts/rebuild_larm26.py` and `scripts/rebuild_castle27.py`. The castle's mainland surroundings, the steps and cobbled ramp from the gate tunnel to the rampart, the gate passage through the castle and all interiors are not modelled; the north tower's height, the east tower's cap, the courtyard levels and the gun positions are estimated.
- Passes 28–74 (the district houses) are applied by their scoped `scripts/rebuild_blockNN.py` scripts; the latest one rebuilds all of them. Yard sides and roofs not seen from the street are estimates, and further generic district volumes remain. Per-pass limitations are listed in [REFINEMENT_28-74.md](REFINEMENT_28-74.md) and the pass notes.
- Passes 75–144 (Kvarnholmen's last houses and yard houses, the mainland and Kalmar slott) are applied by their scoped `scripts/rebuild_blockNN.py` scripts; `scripts/rebuild_block144.py` rebuilds all of them. In Unreal, passes 81–143 are imported in one session by `Unreal/Content/Python/run_union_import.py`. The castle's courtyard level, Kungstrappan's form and several links between rooms are interpreted, the interiors have no ornament beyond simple solids and flat colours, and the mainland has no terrain. Per-pass limitations are listed in [REFINEMENT_75-144.md](REFINEMENT_75-144.md) and the pass notes.
- `Unreal/Content/Python/validate_block82.py` to `validate_block144.py` are generated templates that were never completed: they stop on an undefined street-front rule (`ST`) or on a missing `zones` list, and their street filter is copied from earlier passes. For v0.1.8, a generic street check was run in their place. It sampled every mapped street within 40 m of each pass's changed meshes: 13,684 ground samples and 12,464 capsule sweeps. No pass's own meshes block a street. All 542 samples without ground lie on OpenStreetMap streets beyond the modelled ground. The castle's interior passes and Klapphuset have no street in range.
